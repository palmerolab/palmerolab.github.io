Research updates and milestones

Selected news across projects, mentoring, awards, presentations, and academic service.

  1. Sep 2026

    UDIVA HHOI Challenge and CONTEXTUS at ECCV 2026. Co-organised the challenge and workshop on understanding human interaction in context.

  2. Aug 2026

    New SERMAS paper in the International Journal of Human-Computer Studies. The paper presents a modular architecture for context-aware, socially acceptable interaction with XR agents.

  3. Jun 2026

    Best Talk at MultimodalAI 2026. The presentation “UDIVA HHOI: A Multimodal Dataset for Context-Aware Multi-Actor Interaction Understanding” received the meeting's Best Talk recognition.

  4. Jun 2026

    REACT 2026 challenge paper released. The fourth challenge introduces personalised facial reaction generation and EEG reaction prediction.

  5. Mar 2026

    Paper published on children's perceived emotions. The study examines facial expressions in children aged three to five while they play with apps.

  6. Jan 2026

    PhD completion. My PhD student Germán Barquero successfully defended “On the Move: Towards Realistic and Controllable Human Motion Generation” on 30 January.

  7. Oct 2025

    Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowship. Awarded a five-year fellowship to develop ECASIS and advance socially intelligent systems with hybrid AI.

  8. Oct 2025

    REACT 2025 paper at ACM Multimedia. The third challenge introduced MARS, a large-scale dataset for multiple appropriate facial reaction generation.

  9. Aug 2025

    Paper accepted at ACII 2025. “Enhancing Personality Recognition by Comparing the Predictive Power of Traits, Facets, and Nuances.”

  10. Jun 2025

    First PhD completion. Zejian Zhang successfully defended “Deep Learning Approaches for Human Activity Understanding.”

  11. May 2025

    Extraordinary PhD Thesis Award. Recognised by the Department of Mathematics and Informatics at the University of Barcelona.

  12. Apr 2025

    Two papers accepted at CVPR 2025. Rolling Prediction Models and MixerMDM advance controllable human motion generation.

  13. Mar 2025

    Paper accepted in IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing. Exploring emotion expression recognition in older adults interacting with a virtual coach.
